Chat, share files and jump on a call. Everything is encrypted in your browser, so the server in the middle can't read any of it.

This page is not in a secure context, so encryption and calls are disabled by the browser. Open it via http://localhost:3000 or an https:// address.

  • ⏳ Ephemeral & private No accounts. Disappearing messages and one-click room burn when you're done.
